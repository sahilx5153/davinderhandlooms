Woven on the same looms for generations.

Every piece we sell is made on a traditional handloom, not printed or power-loomed — which means small irregularities in the weave are part of the fabric's character, not a flaw.

Our weavers work in natural fibres — cotton, tussar and linen — dyeing many of the yarns by hand before they ever touch the loom. It's slow work. A single saree can take several days from warp to finished cloth.

We keep the shop small on purpose: every order is packed by us, and if you want to see a fabric up close before you buy, message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you real photos and a video of the piece.
